File 1: uk_clinical_algorithms_audit.csv
This dataset catalogues 16 active and baseline clinical algorithms across NICE, the BNF, ARTP/BTS, and the MHRA.
NG3 (Diabetes in Pregnancy - NICE): Lowers the body mass index (BMI) intervention cut-off from 30 kg/m² to 27 kg/m² for women from ethnic minority groups, recognising elevated metabolic risk at lower weight thresholds.
NG9 (Bronchiolitis in Children - NICE): Records that pulse oximeter optical sensors can overestimate blood oxygen saturation in children with darker skin tones, while noting the guideline committee issued no official correction.
NG12 (Suspected Cancer - NICE): Uses a weighted 7-point checklist score of 3 or higher on suspicious pigmented skin lesions to trigger urgent secondary care referral.
NG25 (Preterm Labour and Birth - NICE): Uses national MBRRACE-UK perinatal mortality data to mandate closer monitoring for women from ethnic minority groups due to higher documented rates of stillbirth.
NG87 (Dementia Assessment - NICE): States that standardised cognitive diagnostic score thresholds must be adjusted and validated when assessing people from minority ethnic backgrounds whose first language is not English.
NG136 (Hypertension in Adults - NICE): Directs clinicians to prescribe a calcium-channel blocker (CCB) rather than an ACE inhibitor as first-line monotherapy for patients of Black African or African-Caribbean family origin of any age.
NG194 (Postnatal Care - NICE): Instructs maternity teams to provide closer clinical monitoring to women and babies from minority ethnic groups based on MBRRACE-UK maternal and infant mortality statistics.
NG201 (Antenatal Care - NICE): Directs additional monitoring and support for pregnant women from minority ethnic backgrounds in response to disproportionate perinatal mortality rates.
NG203 (Chronic Kidney Disease - NICE): Records that historical eGFR prediction equations were poorly validated in UK ethnic minority populations, confirming the removal and deprecation of the 1.159 ethnicity multiplier.
NG207 (Inducing Labour - NICE): Incorporates ethnic disparity data into labour induction assessments, advising that women from minority ethnic backgrounds may benefit from closer clinical monitoring.
NG229 (Fetal Monitoring in Labour - NICE): Recommends customised symphysis fundal height (SFH) charts and ethnic growth curves (e.g. GROW) to adjust fetal centile calculations for small-for-gestational-age babies.
NG238 (Cardiovascular Disease Risk - NICE): Removes earlier guidance permitting automated tool calculation when inputs were missing, noting that QRISK3 overestimates cardiovascular risk if ethnicity and clinical fields remain blank.
NG239 (Vitamin B12 Deficiency - NICE): Notes that individuals of Black ethnicity have a physiologically higher reference range for serum vitamin B12 concentrations than White or Asian populations.
BNF Clozapine (Clozapine ANC Monitoring - BNF / MHRA): Documents mandatory Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) thresholds based on White normative baselines, requiring patients of African descent with Benign Ethnic Neutropenia to obtain a haematologist exemption before starting treatment.
ARTP Spirometry (Race-Neutral Spirometry - ARTP / BTS): Documents the respiratory society position deprecating the historic 10 to 15 per cent lower lung function expectation multiplier for Black individuals in favour of race-neutral GLI 2022 equations.
MHRA Drug Alert (Carbamazepine Safety - MHRA): Mandates genetic screening for the HLA-B*1502 allele before prescribing carbamazepine in patients of Han Chinese, Thai, or other South-East Asian descent to prevent Stevens-Johnson syndrome.

File 2: historical_removals_audit.csv
This dataset records 4 confirmed historical de-adoption and deprecation events identified in clinical guideline revision logs.
NICE NG203 (superseding CG182 / CG73): Deprecation of the 1.159 race multiplier for serum creatinine eGFR calculation, instructing laboratories to report unadjusted values.
ARTP / BTS Position Directive: Deprecation of the historic 10 to 15 per cent reduction multiplier for lung capacity in Black patients, recommending race-neutral GLI reference standards.
NICE CG138 / CG55 Pelvimetry History: Discontinuation of routine radiographic pelvimetry and racialised pelvic shape categories used historically to predict cephalopelvic disproportion during labour.
NICE NG238 (superseding CG181): Removal of advice permitting automated software estimation on blank demographic entries in QRISK, correcting automated risk inflation for Black and South Asian patients.
